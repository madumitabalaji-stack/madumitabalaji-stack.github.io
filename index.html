About

A CV can’t tell the whole story, so here’s the short version.

I trained as a lawyer in India, in an integrated business and law degree. My internships took me through trade and customs disputes, insolvency, merger review, and white-collar investigations, where I drafted an anti-bribery and anti-money-laundering policy for an AI company. Each one taught me the same thing: the interesting work is in how rules are written, and in what they ask of the businesses that have to live with them.

That brought me to Georgetown for my LL.M., and to digital assets. At Bluprynt, I worked on compliance for a digital-asset company: policies and procedures, KYC and customer due diligence, and Travel Rule questions across several countries.

Then I built something. I’m a lawyer, not an engineer, but I used AI-assisted workflows to build the State Stablecoin Atlas on my own, with a pipeline that flags new laws and guidance as they come out. AI sped the work up a great deal. It also invented citations, which is why every entry in the Atlas is checked against the enrolled statute and cited down to the section.

Outside work, you’ll usually find me on a hiking trail or at the gym. I also sing, and I make art whenever I find the time.

Selected work

Founder and MaintainerIndependent project2026 – present

State Stablecoin Atlas

The GENIUS Act left the states with a real role in stablecoin regulation, and the state law underneath it is a patchwork. The Atlas maps it: how each of the 50 states and DC treats money transmission and virtual currency, who regulates it, what license a business needs, and how settled the position is.

Method. Every entry is pinned to the bill number, the session law and the codified section, and verified against official government sources only. A monitoring pipeline checks those sources and flags changes as they happen.
